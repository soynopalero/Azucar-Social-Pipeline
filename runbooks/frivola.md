# Frivola Runbook

> **One-line description:** Monthly drag showcase in the LED Room. **Partner show** — produced by the Frivola team, Azúcar provides the venue + production support. Low-touch on our side.
> **Frequency:** Monthly — 1st Saturday
> **Room:** LED Room
> **Doors:** ~8 PM • **Show:** 9 PM • **Close:** Late
> **Cover:** TBD (Frivola team sets it usually)
> **Age:** 21+
> **Owner:** Frivola team (external) — Azúcar liaison: Pedro

## Quick reference

| | |
|---|---|
| Entertainer lineup | Frivola team handles 100% — we don't book |
| Partner show? | **YES** — they run the show, we run the venue |
| Marketing lead time | Minimal from our side; Frivola team does their own marketing. We just cross-post. |
| Staff needed | Bar, door, sound/lights operator. NO entertainment-side staff needed from us. |

## Timeline (working backward from doors-open)

### T-1 week
- [ ] Confirm with Frivola team that show is still on (no cancellation)
- [ ] Confirm what time their team arrives for setup
- [ ] Confirm any guest performers / special requirements

### T-3 days
- [ ] Cross-post their marketing on @cluboutandabout if they've asked for it
- [ ] Confirm door staff scheduled

### T-1 day
- [ ] **Sound ready** — LED Room sound system functional, mics charged
- [ ] **Lights ready** — LED Room lighting tested, no burnt bulbs
- [ ] **Drag room ready** — clean, mirrors clean, lights on, table space cleared, water + snacks if standard
- [ ] Confirm Frivola team contact for day-of

### T-0 (day of)
- [ ] Frivola team typically arrives ~2 hours before show for setup + sound check
- [ ] Be available for any venue-side issues (HVAC, sound feedback, etc.)
- [ ] Cash drawer set + door staff briefed

## Room setup (LED Room)

- **Lighting:** Frivola team typically runs their own light cues; venue just provides functional LEDs
- **Sound:** Wireless mics + queen playlist routing tested
- **Stage / floor:** Standard LED Room stage configuration
- **Drag room:** Cleaned + stocked per "T-1 day" checklist above
- **Bar:** Standard stock for a Saturday night

## Entertainment booking

- **Not our job.** Frivola team handles all performer bookings, fees, and payouts.
- **Our job:** Make sure the room is ready and the door collects properly. Nothing more.

## Marketing requirements

- **Flyer:** Frivola team produces and distributes their own
- **Cross-post:** If they tag @cluboutandabout or send a flyer, cross-post on our channels (use `azucar-post`)
- **Reservation CTA:** Refer to Frivola team's instructions (they typically don't do reservations through us)

## During event responsibilities

- **Door:** Cover collection (per Frivola team's arrangement)
- **Bar:** Stocked
- **Stage / host:** **NOT US** — Frivola team's host
- **Floor:** 1 person walking the floor
- **Sound / lights:** Operator should coordinate with Frivola team's lead

## Teardown

- Frivola team handles their own equipment teardown
- Bar / floor cleanup is our responsibility
- Door cash settled per partnership agreement with Frivola team
