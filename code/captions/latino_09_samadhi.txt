Healing is part of the party 🌿

Pride days are big, beautiful, and a whole lot of feeling all at once, so we made sure to take care of you. Samadhi Curandera, one of our beloved local curanderas, will be set up at the first aid tent all event long, offering rapid limpias to anyone who needs one.

Maybe you need a quick energetic cleanse, maybe you need a blessing, or maybe you just need a quiet moment to ground yourself between the music and the crowd. Whatever it is, she is there for it. Samadhi is a trusted healer in our community, and she is someone you can reach out to for support well beyond this one day.

Stop by the tent, take a breath, and let yourself be cared for. You deserve that too.
