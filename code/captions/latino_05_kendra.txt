Say hello to our newest queen 💖

Every star has a first night somewhere, and Latino/a Pride is going to be Kendra Montaner's debut here in our area. She may be new to our stage, but she is far from new to the art. Kendra has been performing in her hometown for years, quietly building her craft and her confidence, and now she is ready to introduce herself to the Tri Cities.

This is exactly the kind of moment we live for at Azúcar, opening the stage to young queens who are hungry, talented, and ready for their shot. There is something special about being in the room for someone's very first performance in a new city, and we want you there for it.

Come give Kendra the loud, loving, over the top welcome that every new queen deserves. Be the crowd she remembers for the rest of her career.
