We begin by honoring our ancestors 🪶

Before the music and the dancing take over, we take a moment to honor where we come from. Tonatiuh Xocoyotzin joins us to bless Latino/a Pride with sacred danza, a form of Mexican Indigenous dance that has been carried through generations. He will be accompanied by a drummer and by our own Samadhi Curandera.

This is so much more than a performance. Tonatiuh will share what these dances mean, why they matter, and how they connect us to our roots, especially on a day when we gather to celebrate being fully and proudly ourselves. There is something powerful about holding space for tradition and queerness in the very same breath, and that is exactly what this moment is about.

Come with an open heart and an open mind, and let yourself actually feel it.
