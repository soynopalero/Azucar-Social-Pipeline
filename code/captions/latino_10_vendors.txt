Come for the queens, stay for the goods 🛍️

The stage is only half the fun. Our Latino/a Pride vendor village is fully stacked, so bring a little cash and an empty tote, because you are absolutely going to want to take something home with you.

The Mad Tatter is our resident tattoo artist, ready to send you home with fresh ink and a story to go with it. Belinda's Bloom brings the flowers and that pop of color every good celebration needs. Mad Art Club shows up as your friendly neighborhood art collective right here in the Tri Cities. Bro and Sister Lemonade is sibling powered and seriously sour, the exact kind of refreshing that hits perfectly on a warm Pride afternoon.

Free Mom Hugs will be there too, offering exactly what the name promises, and if you know, you know just how much that can mean to someone. Siren is on hand for photography, so you walk away with a moment worth framing. Rooted Care Collective is bringing the massage therapists, because even your shoulders deserve a little Pride treatment. And Aub's Banana Bread is rounding it out with bread so good we are choosing to keep most of the jokes to ourselves.

Pull up, support local, and treat yourself a little. You earned it.
