La madrina has entered the building 👑

If you have been anywhere near the drag scene in Eastern Washington over the last many years, you already know Frivola. She is the madrina, she is the blueprint, and she is one of the longest reigning queens this side of the state. The résumé speaks for itself. She has spent years hosting Miss Washington Universe and she has sent her girls off to snatch crowns all across the nation.

Frivola does not simply host a show, she runs the entire kingdom. When she takes that microphone, the room falls in line. You sit down, you pay attention, and yes, you tip her well. There is a reason every queen in the area knows her name, and a reason the crowd treats her like royalty.

We are beyond honored to have her gracing our Latino/a Pride stage this year. Come witness the queen who paved the way and somehow still does it better than most.
