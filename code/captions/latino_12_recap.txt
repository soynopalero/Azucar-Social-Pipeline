Two days out. Look at this lineup. 🏳️‍🌈

We are two days away from the largest Latino Pride in Eastern Washington, and the lineup speaks for itself. Folklórico and flamenco, sacred danza, a curandera holding healing space, and a stage full of drag queens both legendary and brand new. Add hosts who keep the whole night moving and a DJ who refuses to let you sit down, and you already have a Saturday worth clearing your calendar for.

Then there is everything around the stage. A full festival with food, drinks, a beer garden, and over fifteen vendors, all ages until 9PM. After that we move inside for another drag show and two rooms of music all night, English hip hop and dance in one and Spanish all night long in the other.

This is our culture, our community, and our joy, all in one place. Two days. Do not make other plans.

Latino/a Pride, presented by Azúcar at Out & About. The largest Latino Pride in Eastern Washington.
Saturday, June 13. Festival outside 4PM to 9PM, all ages. Doors open inside at 9PM, 21 and up.
327 W Lewis St, Pasco.
