Meet your hosts for the night 🌟🏳️‍🌈

Latino/a Pride is being opened and hosted all night long by two of our next generation reinas, Abele Fantasy and Bali, and this is exactly the moment we have been building toward. We love putting the new wave of drag front and center, and these two have absolutely earned the spotlight.

Abele Fantasy first caught our eye at our amateur contest, but do not let the word amateur fool you for a second. She has been performing for years and she is a genuinely talented, polished artist who knows how to command a room. She is local, she is fierce, and she does it all like she has nothing to prove.

Then there is Bali, and honey, you already know. She brings the comedy, the camp, and a Selena tribute that had the whole room screaming the first time she did it. Anyone who caught her at our NextGen show can tell you she was born for this stage.

Put these two together on one mic and you get a whole show inside the show. Come out, tip your hosts well, and support next generation drag the way it deserves.
