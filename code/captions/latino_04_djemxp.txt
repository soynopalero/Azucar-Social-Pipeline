Your in house DJ is ready for you 🎧

The energy of any Azúcar night lives and dies by the booth, and ours is in very good hands. DJ EMXP is our in house DJ, and she knows exactly how to read a room and keep it moving. Expect cumbias, banda, reggaetón, house, and just about everything in between, all blended into one long reason to never leave the dance floor.

She is not new to this either. EMXP has performed all across the state, and her most recent gig had her running the techno stage at the Lollipop raves. If you missed her there, consider this your second chance, because she is bringing that exact same heat to Latino/a Pride.

Wear the comfortable shoes. Once she gets going, your hips are not going to bother asking for your permission.
