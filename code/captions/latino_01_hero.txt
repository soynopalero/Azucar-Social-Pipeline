The biggest Latino Pride in Eastern Washington is back 🏳️‍🌈🌽

Mark your calendar, because Saturday June 13 is going to be a whole day and night of celebration. Latino/a Pride is back, and once again it is the largest Latino Pride in all of Eastern Washington, hosted right here by Azúcar at Out & About.

We start outside with a full festival from 4PM until 9PM. Think food, drinks, a beer garden, vendors, and live entertainment all afternoon long, and it is all ages, so bring the whole familia. Our folklórico dancers, our drag performers, our DJ, and our cultural artists will all be taking the stage throughout the evening.

Then at 9PM we open the doors and take the party inside Azúcar. We kick the night off with another full drag show, and after that we keep it going with two rooms running all night. One room will be playing English hip hop and dance music, and the other will be Spanish music from open to close. Two vibes, one building, zero reasons to go home early.

This is our city, our culture, and our community out loud and unapologetic. Come celebrate with us.

Latino/a Pride, presented by Azúcar at Out & About. The largest Latino Pride in Eastern Washington.
Saturday, June 13. Festival outside 4PM to 9PM, all ages. Doors open inside at 9PM, 21 and up.
327 W Lewis St, Pasco.
