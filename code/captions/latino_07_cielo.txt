Folklórico royalty, right here at home 👢

For the very first time at Azúcar, we get to welcome Ballet Folklórico Cielo de México to our Pride stage. This is a local Tri Cities treasure, a crew of five to ten dancers bringing the traditional dances of northern México to life with the skirts, the boots, and the zapateado that you feel in your chest.

These dancers perform all throughout the year, and their famous annual showcase is honestly one of the most beautiful things you can experience locally. If you have never seen it, put it on your list, because it is the kind of show people talk about for weeks afterward. For now, we are lucky enough to have them at Azúcar, and we could not be more proud to share our stage with them.

They also teach all ages, so if watching them makes you want to learn, they are the ones to find. Come celebrate culture that lives and breathes right here at home.
