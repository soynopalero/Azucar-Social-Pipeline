This one is for our familia 🫶

We are coming up on a whole year of Azúcar, and what a year it has been. A year of late nights, lip syncs, packed dance floors, found family, and more joy than we ever expected to fit into this little corner of Pasco. None of it would be possible without the people behind the scenes.

This post is for Team Azúcar and for every single volunteer who is helping make Latino/a Pride happen. The folks who show up early, stay late, carry the boxes, pour the drinks, and pour their whole hearts into this community are the real highlight of the entire thing. We see you, we appreciate you, and we genuinely could not do any of this without you.

From our familia to yours, thank you. Now let us go celebrate the right way, together.
