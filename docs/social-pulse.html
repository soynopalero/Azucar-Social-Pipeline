The short version

Needs a decision We post 4× more than in spring, and each post now reaches 60–80% fewer people.

Instagram went from about 60 posts a month to about 280. Facebook views per post fell from about 450 to about 90. Past about 6 posts a day, extra posts mostly compete with each other.

Biggest opportunity Reels reach 3× more people than photos. We nearly stopped making them.

A typical Reel reaches 505 people and gets 22 interactions. A typical photo reaches 174 and gets 5. We made 21 Reels in March and 2 in September.

How many posts a day is enough?

Instagram: total reach on a typical day, by how many posts went out that day

Jun 1 – Sep 20, 105 days. The number under each bar is reach per post. Totals add up each post's reach, so a person who saw 3 posts counts 3 times. That makes the busy days look better than they really are.

What this says: going from 1–2 posts a day to about 6 roughly triples the day's reach. After that, adding more posts barely moves the total and cuts each post's reach in half. Beyond about 6 a day, the extra posts are mostly work with little to show for it.

What to decide

Your callCap posting at about 6 a day per platform.

We'd keep nearly all of today's daily reach, stop flyers from competing with each other, and free up the time that currently goes into extra posts. For big weekends, cut the older, smaller events first.

Your callRequire one Reel for every event.

A 15–30 second clip from the last edition, a performer or the room. Reels reach 3× as many people and bring in the most interactions per post.

About these numbers

  • Reach is how many different people saw a post. Views (Facebook) counts every time it was seen. Meta replaced Facebook "impressions" with views in November 2025.
  • Account-level daily history only started on Sep 18, 2026. Follower trends will get more reliable with each week.
  • Instagram history covers 1,743 posts back to 2017. Facebook covers 1,297 posts from Feb 26, 2026.
  • Collected every morning at 6 am by our automated pull. Stored in data/insights/ in the Azucar-Social-Pipeline repo.
